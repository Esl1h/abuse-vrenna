# Free sound pack

Credits for everything in `data/sfx/` and `data/music/`: 73 effects and 12
tracks. Most of it is CC0 or public domain and asks for nothing. Two sources
ask for something, and both are given here.

**Horror Sound Effects Library**, by Little Robot Sound Factory, is CC-BY 3.0.
That licence requires the author to be credited wherever the sounds are
redistributed:

> Horror Sound Effects Library by Little Robot Sound Factory, CC-BY 3.0,
> https://opengameart.org/content/horror-sound-effects-library

The author also asks for a link to their own website. A 2019 comment on the
page reports that the address now belongs to someone else, so the page on
OpenGameArt is linked instead.

**Dark Ambience Loop**, by Iwan Gabovitch (qubodup), is offered under CC-BY 3.0,
GPL 3.0 and GPL 2.0. It is taken here under GPL 2.0, which asks for no credit,
but the author asks for one:

> Dark Ambience Loop by Iwan Gabovitch qubodup.net

The licence of each source was read on its page on 2026-10-19. Which file comes
from which source is in `data/MANIFEST.toml`, one entry per file.

The file names are the ones the 1995 Lisp asks for. The sounds are
not the 1995 sounds: those belong to Bobby Prince, are not
redistributable, and are in no package here. The Original mode plays
his, from the data the player installs; this pack is what the
Remastered mode plays instead.

Built by `tools/free-sfx/build-pack.py` (effects) and
`tools/free-sfx/build-music.py` (tracks), which hold the mapping.
